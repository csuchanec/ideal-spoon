Overall Manager Assessment

A retrospective rubric in grade points out of 100—not a probability or causal skill estimate.

Overall = .35 P + .35 E + .20 J + .10 S

Fixed shares: team performance 35%, lineup execution 35%, expectation-aware judgment 20%, roster stewardship 10%. Missing management evidence uses an explicit 75-point reference anchor at its unchanged share. Missing production makes the overall unavailable. Shares are never redistributed.

Season and historical assessments average compatible raw weekly overall observations and the same weekly component contributions, including declared anchors. They do not average letters or apply nonlinear formulas to averaged inputs. Sensitivity varies missing components over their stated ranges at fixed shares (judgment 50–100, other components 0–100); it is not a confidence interval.

P · Relative team performance

all-play = 100 × (wins + .5 × ties) / other league teams z = (points − weekly league mean) / weekly standard deviation scoring magnitude = 50 + 50 × tanh(z / 2) P = .5 × all-play + .5 × scoring magnitude

An equal-score cohort receives 50. This describes production; it is not the whole management assessment.

E · Legal lineup execution

R = max(0, supported optimal points O − actual points A) q = strongest feasible single-swap support: clip(positive bench points / 10, 0, 1) D = 10 × occupied lineup slot count E = clip(75 + 25 q − 100 R / D, 0, 100)

The denominator is a fixed per-slot scoring reference for the whole lineup: unrelated starter scoring cannot dilute a mistake, but a single contested position does not represent the entire lineup. Interchangeable decision slots, including FLEX/superflex chains, remain available as worked diagnostics. No alternatives means zero regret and no competition credit.

Retain actual starters, including zero/negative outcomes. Exclude reserves, known bench byes and hypothetical replacements without positive recorded participation. This is an evidence filter, not an inferred injury. No choice and a zero-point bench option both receive zero competition credit; playable scoring alternatives introduce continuous credit against a fixed 10-point reference, not an automatic 25-point jump. The credit is not divided by actual performance or summed across slots; stronger actual scoring cannot reduce it. Exact global legal regret remains the opportunity comparison. Participation does not prove availability at lineup lock.

J · Expectations and outcomes

C = legal crowd lineup maximizing strictly prior expectation indices U = sum(.8 × prior starting percentage + .2 × prior ownership percentage) c = clip((U(C) − U(A)) / (100 × max(crowd replacement count, 1)), 0, 1) J_assessed = 75 + 25 c tanh((A − C) / 8) J_effective = 75 + starting-rate coverage × (J_assessed − 75)

Missing ownership falls back to starting percentage alone with explicit coverage. Unknown actual starters are held fixed in the crowd reference; unknown bench expectations are excluded, not assigned zero. Known decisions remain assessed at their declared partial coverage. No prior rates means unassessed judgment. Actual selections that maximize the expectation objective count as the reference, preventing tie-only credit. Crowd following receives 75 even if an unexpected bench breakout creates hindsight regret. Stronger prior expectation gaps scale both successful and costly dissent; near-tie preferences earn smaller adjustments. Ownership and starting rates are not scoring probabilities.

Judgment does not subtract hindsight regret a second time. Execution and judgment still share actual outcomes, so they are not independent evidence. Judgment's theoretical missing-evidence range is 50–100, symmetrically centered on 75; partial and historical point rankings require comparable coverage.

S · Roster stewardship

net acquisition = supported optimal after-roster points − supported optimal before-roster points acquisition score = 75 + 25 tanh(net acquisition / 8) effective acquisition = 75 + reliability × (acquisition score − 75) waiver score = 75 − 25 tanh(verified marginal gain × expectation index / 8) S = .5 × acquisition subscore + .5 × waiver subscore

Reconstruct successful transaction-linked adds and drops jointly, keeping occupied slots fixed. Missing drop/scoring evidence makes acquisition unassessed. Signed legal frontier value—not pickup bench points—is credited. Deployment is already assessed in execution/judgment, not a second acquisition bonus. Downloaded event timestamps are compared with the reported NFL team's scheduled kickoff in US Eastern time (converted to UTC). Successful events strictly before kickoff resolve scheduled timing; they do not establish historical injury knowledge or counterfactual claim success. Reliability remains 0.5 when scheduled timing cannot be resolved for every event, a rubric convention rather than a calibrated probability.

A confirmed feasible pre-lock waiver pool with no supported improvement earns 85 for justified patience. Unknown acquisition or waiver evidence uses its explicit 75 reference anchor. No recorded moves is not automatically good or bad: weekly/current ownership snapshots do not establish which alternative claims would have succeeded before kickoff. Those grading limits do not mean the importer download is incomplete. Snapshots alone cannot justify injury-drop recommendations or penalties.

Full hindsight remains separate

unused points = exact legal optimum − actual points capture = 100 × actual / optimum, when optimum > 0

Full retrospective opportunity and the supported grading reference are different comparisons. Preserve both: do not hide raw missed points by excluding uncertain candidates from grading. Both respect full eligibility, unique players and signed scoring.
