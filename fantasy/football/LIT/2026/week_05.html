Overall Manager Assessment: What Goes Into the Grade

One balanced retrospective assessment: P35 / E35 / J20 / S10. Scores are points out of 100, not probabilities.

P · Team Performance

League-relative all-play position and scoring magnitude describe production. They contribute to the balanced grade without replacing management.

E · Lineup Execution

Supported legal alternatives measure marginal points left unused. Actual zero/negative starters remain; hypothetical reserves, known byes and unverified nonparticipants do not create grading mistakes. No alternative player set demonstrates competence, not perfection.

J · Expectation-aware Judgment

Strictly prior starting and ownership rates contextualize actual-versus-crowd outcomes and supported regret. Good following can be competent; costly high-confidence benching is penalized. Execution and judgment overlap intentionally and are not independent evidence.

S · Roster Stewardship

Signed joint legal roster-frontier improvement evaluates acquisitions, not pickup bench points. Verified waiver opportunity can evaluate action or justified patience; missing pre-lock access and timing evidence stays explicit.

Missing management evidence uses a declared 75-point reference anchor without changing its fixed share. Anchors are not evidence of competent decisions.
