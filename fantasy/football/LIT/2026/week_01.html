How Manager Grades Work

Weekly and season grades answer different questions. Lineup execution remains visible as its own metric, while matchup luck and hindsight-only waiver options are excluded.

Weekly Management Grade

Lineup decisions 60%, same-week transaction deployment 25%, competitive output 15%. When there is no transaction evidence, its weight is redistributed. This asks how well the manager handled the choices available that week.

Season Manager Rating

League-relative lineup management 35%, roster construction 30%, transaction value 20%, and points performance 15%. Each component shows its underlying measure and league rank. Missing evidence is omitted and adaptability is descriptive until it can be measured reliably.

Early-Season Confidence

The score is the aggregate of evidence observed so far; it is not reduced because the sample is small. Weeks 1–2 are Provisional, Weeks 3–5 are Developing, and Week 6 onward is Established.

Lineup Execution

Actual starter points ÷ best legal lineup points × 100. It explains start/sit conversion but is no longer presented as the entire management grade.
