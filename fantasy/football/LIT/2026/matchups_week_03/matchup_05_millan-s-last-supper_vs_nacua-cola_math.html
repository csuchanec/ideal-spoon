Overall Manager Assessment

A retrospective rubric in grade points out of 100—not a probability or causal skill estimate.

Overall = .35 P + .35 E + .20 J + .10 S

Fixed shares: team performance 35%, lineup execution 35%, expectation-aware judgment 20%, roster stewardship 10%. Missing management evidence uses an explicit 75-point reference anchor at its unchanged share. Missing production makes the overall unavailable. Shares are never redistributed.

Season and historical assessments average compatible raw weekly overall observations and the same weekly component contributions, including declared anchors. They do not average letters or apply nonlinear formulas to averaged inputs. Sensitivity varies missing components over their stated ranges at fixed shares (judgment 50–100, other components 0–100); it is not a confidence interval.

P · Relative team performance

all-play = 100 × (wins + .5 × ties) / other league teams z = (points − weekly league mean) / weekly standard deviation scoring magnitude = 50 + 50 × tanh(z / 2) P = .5 × all-play + .5 × scoring magnitude

An equal-score cohort receives 50. This describes production; it is not the whole management assessment.

E · Legal lineup execution

R = max(0, supported optimal points O − actual points A) q = strongest feasible single-swap support: clip(positive bench points / 10, 0, 1) D = 10 × occupied lineup slot count E = clip(75 + 25 q − 100 R / D, 0, 100)
