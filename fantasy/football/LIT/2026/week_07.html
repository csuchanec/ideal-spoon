P · Team Performance

League-relative all-play position and scoring magnitude describe production. They contribute to the balanced grade without replacing management.

E · Lineup Execution

Supported legal alternatives measure marginal points left unused. Actual zero/negative starters remain; hypothetical reserves, known byes and unverified nonparticipants do not create grading mistakes. No alternative player set demonstrates competence, not perfection.

J · Expectation-aware Judgment

Prior starting/ownership rates and labeled draft fallbacks contextualize actual-versus-crowd outcomes and supported regret. A last-resort same-week starting reference is retrospective, not a pre-week forecast. Good following can be competent; costly high-confidence benching is penalized. Execution and judgment overlap intentionally and are not independent evidence.

S · Roster Stewardship

Signed joint legal roster-frontier improvement evaluates acquisitions, not pickup bench points. Observed unrostered alternatives are compared with the owned optimal lineup, including bench options, to assess roster sufficiency and justified patience. Unknown competing auction bids do not block scoring; timing and availability limits stay explicit.
