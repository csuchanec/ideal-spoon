How Manager Grades Work

Weekly and season grades answer different questions. Lineup execution remains visible as its own metric, while matchup luck and hindsight-only waiver options are excluded.

Weekly Management Grade

Lineup execution 30%, roster and waiver management 20%, all-play position 25%, and scoring performance 25%. Roster and waiver management evaluates actual additions and whether qualified available-player upgrades existed against the team's bench. Standing pat can score well when no meaningful upgrade was missed; inactivity is not automatically good or bad. Matchup luck remains context only.

Season Manager Rating

Lineup management 20%, roster construction 25%, post-draft move impact 20%, and competitive strength 35%. Move impact uses starter value above replacement and move-assisted wins. Competitive strength combines all-play results, scoring, actual wins, and dominant wins. The label is provisional early, but the score reflects only the season played so far.

Early-Season Confidence

The score is the aggregate of evidence observed so far; it is not reduced because the sample is small. Weeks 1–2 are Provisional, Weeks 3–5 are Developing, and Week 6 onward is Established.

Lineup Execution

Actual starter points ÷ best legal lineup points × 100. It explains start/sit conversion but is no longer presented as the entire management grade.
