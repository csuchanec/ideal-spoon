Historical Manager Rating

The rating pools underlying evidence across the selected window rather than averaging annual grades. Lineup management is 25%, team building is 25% (equal parts drafting and post-draft starter production), luck-adjusted competitive strength is 20%, regular-season finishing is 10%, and completed-season résumé is 20%. Titles, podiums, playoffs, best records, and points titles help the résumé; last-place finishes hurt it. Fixed evidence rubrics determine every score, so league rank never forces a manager into a high grade. Roster ceiling, raw scoring, actual record, and luck remain visible diagnostics without being counted twice. Missing evidence is reweighted across available components. Confidence describes sample size and never changes the score.
