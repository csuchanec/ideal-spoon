How the rating works

The rating pools underlying evidence across the selected window rather than averaging annual grades. Lineup management is 20%; 80% of hindsight-optimal points is competent and 90% is A-level. Team building is 25%, combining draft return, post-draft starter production, roster strength, and game-changing move value. Competitive strength is 25%, combining weekly all-play performance, actual scoring, win rate, and dominant victories while reporting schedule luck separately. Regular-season finishing is 10% and completed-season résumé is 20%. Résumé rates are compared with the neutral opportunity supplied by each season's league size, so titles, podiums, playoffs, best records, and points titles help while last-place finishes hurt. Fixed evidence rubrics determine every score; league rank never changes the grade. Missing evidence is reweighted across available components, and confidence describes sample size without changing the score.
