How Manager Grades Work

Grades evaluate lineup conversion and season-long management process. They do not grade raw points scored, matchup luck, or hindsight waiver options as if they were known before kickoff.

Weekly grade

Actual starter points ÷ best legal lineup points × 100. The percentage is capped to the 45–100 grading range. Transaction outcomes are displayed as evidence but do not double-count the same lineup result.

Season grade

Starts from 85, applies 70% of cumulative lineup efficiency above or below 90%, then adjusts for moved-away-player regret, trend, exceptional efficiency, and the latest weekly grade. It is cumulative through the report week.
